Privacy Policy — Video Factory

Last updated: November 18, 2026

Video Factory is a personal tool operated by the owner of the YouTube channels "Curious Leo" / "Leo Curioso". It is used only by that owner to upload videos to the owner's own YouTube channels.

What data the app accesses

  • Through Google OAuth, the app requests permission to upload videos (youtube.upload) and to read basic channel information (youtube.readonly) for the channels the owner authorizes.
  • It reads only the channel name and ID to confirm which channel was connected.

How the data is used and stored

  • The authorization token is stored as an encrypted secret in the owner's private GitHub repository and is used only to upload the owner's videos with their title, description and tags.
  • The app does not collect, sell or share personal data, and it does not access data from any other person or channel.
  • The app's use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements. It also follows the YouTube Terms of Service and the Google Privacy Policy.

How to revoke access

Access can be removed at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions. Revoking it deletes the app's ability to upload videos.
