Leo Curioso · Fun science Shorts in English and Spanish

Curious Leo is a YouTube channel where a curious lab pup calculates surprising things: how many balloons would lift a whale, how many soccer balls would fill the Earth, and more.

Video Factory (the app)

Video Factory is a private tool used only by the channel owner to produce these videos and upload them to the owner's own YouTube channels through the official YouTube Data API. It is not offered to other users.
